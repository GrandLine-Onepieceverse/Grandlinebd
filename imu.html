Taib Hasan Alif is a man cloaked in mystery and wrapped in silence — a sovereign whose very presence alters the air around him. Calm and deliberate, he moves like a shadow across the world stage, never raising his voice, yet making kings kneel and nations fall in line. Behind his measured gaze lies a mind sharper than any blade, a strategist who weaves political threads like a spider spins its web. He has built empires without drawing a sword, ended rebellions with a single glance, and restructured the global order through whispers alone.

He speaks rarely, but when he does, his words become law. Those who hear them are often left questioning if they acted by choice—or command. Even within the sacred halls of Mary Geoise, where power breeds arrogance, his influence is absolute. The Celestial Dragons speak his name in hushed tones, and even the Gorosei once bowed before his vision. What little is known of his past is cloaked in erased records and vanished testimonies. No one truly knows how he rose to power, nor how he maintains it. He does not need to display strength—because the world already assumes he has it. And in a world built on fear and legacy, that is more terrifying than any weapon.

"The light of freedom must be extinguished... for the sake of peace."
