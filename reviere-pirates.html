REVIERE PIRATES

The Dream Weavers of North Blue

Masters of illusion and psychological warfare

Reviere Pirates

"Where Dreams and Nightmares Collide"

Crew Size: 9 members
Territory: North Blue
Total Bounty: 309,000,000 Berries

The Reverie Pirates, emerging from the foggy coasts of the North Blue, are led by the quiet yet enigmatic Captain Rayan. Calm in demeanor but sharp in execution, Rayan has gathered a crew known for their subtle movements and unpredictable goals. They rarely seek direct conflict, instead favoring precision strikes and well-timed escapes. Though not openly hostile, their growing influence and tactical intelligence make them a crew worth watching. Marines are advised to proceed with discretion and report any activity related to the Reverie Pirates.
