Riyadh

The Shadow Crown of the World Government

Unknown Age
Mary Geoise Location
Supreme Authority

Where Taib Hasan Alif leads with vision and diplomacy, Riyadh is the blade that guards the dream from the darkness. Unseen by the world, unknown to most of history, he is the silent guardian who ensures the survival of the world order—even if it means sacrificing his own name, reputation, and humanity.

He does not sit in thrones or address grand assemblies. Riyadh works from the dark corridors behind the marbled halls of Mary Geoise, striking down threats before they can even be named. He is the "Shadow Head" — the one who carries out what cannot be officially sanctioned. Without Riyadh, Taib’s grand design could crumble. Without Taib’s light, Riyadh’s darkness would consume him. Together, they are two sides of the same unbreakable rule: Order at any cost.

"For peace to shine, someone must bear the weight of every necessary sin."
