About Grandline Bangladesh

Welcome to Grandline Bangladesh, the first and largest One Piece community in Bangladesh. We are a group of passionate fans dedicated to celebrating the rich world of One Piece created by Eiichiro Oda.

Our community brings together fans from all across Bangladesh to discuss the manga, anime, theories, and to participate in various One Piece themed events and activities.

Whether you're a long-time fan or just starting your journey in the world of One Piece, Grandline Bangladesh offers a welcoming space for all enthusiasts to connect, share, and grow together.

Join us as we navigate the Grand Line, discover the mysteries of the One Piece world, and forge friendships that will last a lifetime!
