Syrex Pirates

"Death Comes in Many Forms"

Crew Size: 13 members
Territory: North Blue
Total Bounty: 472,000,000 Berries

The Syrex Pirates, a rising crew from the North Blue, are led by the bold and daring Captain Zafi. Known for their fast-moving ship and swift raids, the Syrex Pirates favor speed and surprise over drawn-out battles. Captain Zafi, though relatively new to the seas, has already earned a reputation for his unshakable confidence and fearless leadership. The crew's movements suggest a hunger for adventure and notoriety, making them unpredictable and increasingly bold. Marines are advised to monitor ports and trade routes in the region, as this crew may be seeking to expand their reach.
