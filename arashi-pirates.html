ARASHI PIRATES

The Storm Bringers of East Blue

A crew known for their mastery of weather and sea navigation

Arashi Pirates

"Riders of the Storm"

Crew Size: 9 members
Territory: East Blue
Total Bounty: 232,000,000 Berries

The Arashi Pirates have recently surfaced in the East Blue, led by the elusive and mysterious Captain Taj. Little is known about his origins or true intentions, but sightings confirm a growing crew of skilled and loyal individuals rallying behind his command. Unlike other pirate groups, the Arashi Pirates operate with precision and rarely leave traces behind, suggesting a level of discipline uncommon among most East Blue crews. While not currently classified as a major threat, their potential remains under close observation. Any information regarding their movements is to be reported immediately
