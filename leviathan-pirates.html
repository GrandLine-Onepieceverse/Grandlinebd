The Leviathan Pirates

"From the Depths We Rise"

Crew Size: 5 members
Territory: North Blue
Total Bounty: 138,000,000 Berries

The Leviathan Pirates, hailing from the North Blue, are quickly rising in infamy under the leadership of their young and ambitious captain, Rose. Known more for her clever tactics and inspiring presence than brute force, Captain Rose has united a diverse crew with a shared dream that stretches beyond treasure. Though not yet among the most dangerous crews, their growing reputation and unpredictable actions have drawn the attention of Marine HQ. Caution is advised, as their potential threat level continues to climb.
