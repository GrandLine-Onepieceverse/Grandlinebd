HELL'S TENANT

The Demonic Pirates of East Blue

A mysterious crew rumored to have made deals with demons for supernatural abilities

Hell's Tenant

"Those Who Dwell in Darkness"

Crew Size: 6 members
Territory: East Blue
Total Bounty: 455,003,000 Berries

The Hell’s Tenant Pirates have recently stirred waves in the East Blue under the command of the fierce and headstrong Captain Uzi. Known for his short temper and unrelenting combat style, Uzi has gathered a crew that thrives in chaos and confrontation. Their flag is feared in smaller towns and outposts, where their name alone often sparks panic. Though not yet a major threat on a global scale, the Hell’s Tenant Pirates have demonstrated the potential to rise quickly. All Marine personnel in the East Blue are instructed to remain alert and exercise caution when patrolling pirate-heavy waters.
