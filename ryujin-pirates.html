Ryujin Pirates

"The Sea Dragons of the East"

Crew Size: 12 members
Territory: East Blue
Total Bounty: 479,000,000 Berries

The Ryujin Pirates are deemed an increasing threat to the stability of the seas. Under the command of Capitan Sami, the crew has shown exceptional growth in strength, influence, and territorial presence. With members specializing in navigation, combat, intelligence, and medicine, they are to be approached with extreme caution. Any sightings should be reported immediately to the nearest Marine base. Their bounties reflect the danger they pose to world peace.

The Ryugami

From the abyss of forgotten legends, it rises… Born from the wreckage of ancient warships and bathed in the fury of forgotten seas, Ryugami isn’t just a ship, it’s a beast!. A twisted fusion of Pluton’s indestructible armor, the ghostly presence of Thriller Bark, the swift soul of Oro Jackson, and the wrath of a sea king, it sails as if possessed, answering to no captain, only to destiny. Its obsidian-plated hull gleams like dragon scales in moonlight. The sails, torn yet alive, whisper the names of the fallen. Cannons fire not with powder, but with cursed blaze, searing through both ships and souls. Beneath its deck lie ancient relics, corridors haunted by echoing footsteps, and a logbook that writes itself in crimson ink. While a massive dragon coils through the stormy sky, forever bound to this cursed warship, Ryugami is more than a vessel a nightmare that sails under a Jolly Roger soaked in vengeance. Some say it was born from the depths of the abyss, others claim it was a dragon’s wrath made into a ship—but one truth remains: No one who faces Ryugami lives to tell the tale. 🌊 Will you challenge the storm… or bow before the legend? ⚔️🔥
